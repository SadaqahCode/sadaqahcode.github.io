Our core value

Our work begins with intention.

قُلْ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحْيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

“Say, ‘Indeed, my prayer, my rites of sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds.’”

Surah Al-An'am (6:162)

This verse is the foundation of our work. We believe our time, knowledge, skills, and efforts are part of our lives, and we strive to dedicate them to Allah.

Through Sadaqah Code, we aim to make our work in technology a means of benefit for the Ummah — building useful tools, sharing knowledge, and contributing to lasting good.

What is Sadaqah Code?

Sadaqah Code is an Islamic open-source initiative dedicated to building high-quality, free, and accessible digital tools for Muslims worldwide.

We believe technology should serve people, not exploit them. Our mission is to create software that brings real benefit to the Muslim community while remaining transparent, accessible, and grounded in Islamic values.

Why “Sadaqah Code”?

Sadaqah Jariyah is an ongoing charity that continues to benefit others. We see open-source work as one possible means of contributing to lasting benefit.

  • Build useful tools that people can continue to use.
  • Share knowledge that others can learn from and build upon.
  • Make software open to improvement and collaboration.

Code as a means of ongoing charity.

What we stand for

Our principles shape what we build, how we build it, and how we treat the people who use it.

01

Open Source

We make our projects available for people to study, use, modify, and contribute to under their respective open-source licenses.

02

Privacy & Data Minimization

No advertising or sale of user data. We avoid unnecessary data collection and use local storage or offline functionality where practical.

03

Islamic Values

We aim to handle the Quran, Sunnah, and Islamic content with care, accuracy, and respect.

04

Free & Accessible

Our goal is to keep useful tools accessible without advertising or unnecessary barriers.

05

Respectful Technology

We avoid manipulative design and unnecessary engagement loops. Technology should respect people's time, attention, and dignity.

06

Community Contribution

We welcome shared knowledge, thoughtful feedback, and contributions from people with different skills and backgrounds.

Above all, we value lasting benefit — work intended to remain useful beyond its first release.

Who we are

Sadaqah Code was founded and is maintained by the FlagoDNA team, which leads the technical development and stewardship of the initiative.

Sadaqah Code provides the open-source identity, mission, and principles that guide this work. The community is welcome to contribute, collaborate, and benefit from the projects.

  1. FlagoDNA leads technical development and maintenance.
  2. Sadaqah Code carries the mission and guiding principles.
  3. The community contributes, collaborates, and benefits.
