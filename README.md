## 📖 About Sadaqah Code

**Sadaqah Code** is an Islamic open-source initiative dedicated to building high-quality, free, and accessible digital tools for Muslims worldwide.

We believe technology should serve people, not exploit them. Our mission is to create software that brings real benefits to the Muslim community while remaining transparent, accessible, and grounded in Islamic values.

Our work focuses on:

- **Islamic Technology:** Building digital tools that support Muslims in their daily lives.
- **Open Source:** Making software available for people to study, use, modify, and contribute to.
- **Accessibility:** Keeping useful tools accessible without unnecessary barriers.
- **Privacy:** Respecting users and minimizing unnecessary data collection.
- **Community:** Encouraging collaboration, shared knowledge, and collective contribution.

---

## 🤲 Why Sadaqah Code?

In Islam, **Sadaqah Jariyah** refers to ongoing charity — good that continues to benefit others beyond the initial act.

We see open-source software as one possible way to contribute to lasting benefit:

- Write useful code → others can continue benefiting from it.
- Share knowledge → others can learn and build upon it.
- Create open tools → the community can improve and maintain them.
- Make technology accessible → more people can benefit without unnecessary barriers.

The value of our efforts ultimately rests with Allah.

**Code as a means of ongoing charity.**

## 🎯 What We Stand For

### Open Source

We believe software should be transparent and accessible.

- Publicly available source code.
- Open collaboration and contributions.
- No unnecessary proprietary lock-in.
- Respect for open-source licenses.

### Privacy & Data Minimization

Users deserve control and respect over their data.

- No advertising or sale of user data.
- No unnecessary accounts or data collection.
- Minimal operational telemetry where needed.
- Local storage and offline functionality where practical.

### Islamic Values

Technology should be developed with an understanding of the values it serves.

- Respect for the Quran and Sunnah.
- Careful handling of Islamic content and references.
- Tools designed to support beneficial practices.
- Technology aligned with Islamic ethics.

### Free & Accessible

Useful technology should not be unnecessarily restricted.

- Free access to our community tools.
- No advertisements.
- No unnecessary paywalls.
- Accessible to Muslims worldwide.

### Respectful Technology

Technology should respect human attention, time, and dignity.

- No manipulative design patterns.
- No unnecessary engagement loops.
- No exploitation of user attention.
- Functionality that serves people rather than controlling them.

### Community Contribution

We believe useful software becomes stronger through shared knowledge and collaboration.

- Contributions from developers and non-developers are welcome.
- Community feedback helps guide improvements.
- Knowledge and resources are shared openly.
- Projects are built for the benefit of the wider Ummah.
